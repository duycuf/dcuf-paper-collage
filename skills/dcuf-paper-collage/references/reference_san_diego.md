# r5 San Diego Zoo "Africa Rocks" — frame analysis

This is a 15-second torn-paper photo-collage animation. Real photo cut-outs (boombox, meerkats, a bateleur eagle, hyrax, klipspringer, acacias, B&W rocks) sit on hand-torn magazine paper with halftone dots and newsprint strips. Everything is animated on twos (12 drawings/s, every image held 2 frames, confirmed by frame differencing), so it reads as tactile stop-motion rather than smooth After Effects. There is no hard cut until the last second: the "camera" does one continuous trip. It pushes into the boombox speaker, goes through a 4-fold mirrored kaleidoscope tunnel, bursts through a torn hexagon hole into an eagle flight with a camera roll, rolls back to the meerkat savanna, and tilts up behind a meerkat that rockets on a plume of torn paper streamers. That tilt lands with an expo-out on a red/blue extruded title. The ending is a sticker-style page peel to a spray-paint wordmark on a torn strip, then a 0.58 s flicker through 8 alternate-universe logo styles. The energy comes from saturated cyan/red/mustard/chartreuse paper on white, white fibrous torn edges with soft drop shadows, replacement-animation pop-ups and constant scale changes.

## Devices
- **Stop-motion on twos for everything** (code; 12 drawings/s throughout. Only the 13.83-14.42 flicker runs on ones and threes.): Every layer, camera move and zoom updates only on even frames (24 fps playback, 12 unique drawings per second). Frame differencing shows about 0.1 on every odd frame across the whole clip except the final flicker. Holds still carry 1-2 px of per-drawing layer jitter (paper boil). — RECIPE: Render the canvas at 24 fps but quantise animation time: tq = floor(frame/2)*2/24. Derive all tweens, camera transforms and noise seeds from tq. Per drawing, offset each cut-out layer by a seeded random ±1.5 px and rotate it ±0.3°, and re-seed the paper-grain overlay. Post-process any Seedance clip the same way: keep every other frame and duplicate it.
- **Torn-paper layers with white fibre edge + die-cut photo stickers** (both; static material; boils on twos): Every shape is a torn magazine-paper polygon with a 2-6 px ragged white core along its edge. Photo objects have a 3-4 px clean white die-cut border plus a soft drop shadow. Fills carry halftone dot screens and newsprint text. — RECIPE: Assets: Nano Banana Pro stickers isolated on flat grey or white, then remove_background. Code torn edge: generate the polygon with midpoint displacement (3 octaves, amplitude 6/3/1.5 px). Draw it first in #F2F1F2 expanded by 3 px, then the coloured paper inset with a multiply paper-grain texture. Sticker border: alpha dilate 3 px in white. Shadow: rgba(20,20,40,0.28), blur 8, offset (4,6). Halftone: 6 px pitch dot grid with radius 1.6 px in white or the paper colour, clipped to the stripe.
- **3-drawing drop-in with a hard landing** (code; 0.25 to 0.42 s (3 drawings), settle at 0.5 s, landing on the beat): The hero object falls from above in 3 drawings: half above frame, mid-air rotated -8°, landed level. Then one settle drawing at 1.03x scale, back to 1.0. No bounce. — RECIPE: Keyframes in tq: y = -60 %, -20 %, final; rot = -4°, -8°, 0°. The settle drawing sets scale 1.03 and y+1 % for one drawing. Land the 0° drawing exactly on a kick.
- **Whack-a-mole replacement pop-ups** (code; stagger 0.17-0.33 s; corner peek about 0.25 s up, 0.5 s hold, 0.25 s down, re-rise 0.4 s later): Small characters or objects appear or disappear in a single drawing, or rise from behind a ground layer in 2 drawings. A giant close-up version peeks up from a frame corner, sinks, and re-rises closer. — RECIPE: Draw cut-outs behind a torn ground layer, used as the mask. Visibility is a step function on tq. Rise is 2 drawings: y from +100 % to +40 % to 0 % of the object's height. The corner giant is scaled to 45 % of frame height, anchored at the bottom-right, and the second rise uses 1.25x scale.
- **Accelerating push into a detail + paper iris** (code; 1.3 s (2.67 to 3.96 s), ease-in; diff 12 rising to 90): The camera zooms ever faster into one small detail (the speaker cone). Inside it, red paper triangles rotate and unfold like a camera iris around a cyan/yellow torn hexagon, until it fills the frame. — RECIPE: scale = exp(1.8*u^2) with u in 0..1 over 1.3 s, giving about 6x. The camera centre lerps from (50 %, 50 %) to the target with the same u. Iris: 7 red #E90208 torn triangles placed radially. Each drawing adds 12° rotation, and the triangles grow from r = 0.1 to r = 0.6 of the target. Hand off to the kaleidoscope at full fill.
- **4-fold mirror kaleidoscope tunnel** (code; about 2 s; scale doubles every about 0.35 s at a constant rate): Octagonal concentric rings of polar-warped photo bands (grass, cloud sky, red paper) with stickers (boomboxes, meerkats, lightning bolts, paper squares). Mirrored on X and Y, with no rotation and an infinite zoom-in where new rings bloom from the centre. — RECIPE: Build a source tile (NBP collage assets placed in one quadrant). Render each drawing to an offscreen canvas and draw it into the 4 quadrants with scale(±1,±1) around the centre. Infinite zoom: draw 6 nested copies at scale 2^(i - phase), where phase += 1/(0.35*12) per drawing. Each copy is clipped by an octagon with a torn white rim. Add 8-fold diagonal mirroring for the inner rings only.
- **Cut-outs flying out of the tunnel at camera** (code; 7 drawings (about 0.6 s) each, overlapping, starting 0.7 s before the transition): Animal (sticker) cut-outs emerge from inside a ring, scale up fast and drift outward off the left/right edges, as if they fly past the lens. — RECIPE: Per object: scale 1, 1.25, 1.55, 1.95, 2.4, 2.9, 3.5. x offset grows ±(4, 8, 14, 22, 32, 45, 60) % of width. Keep the white sticker border width constant in screen pixels so it does not fatten. Also swap each object's pose image once mid-flight.
- **Burst-through hole (window zoom-through)** (code; 3-4 drawings (0.25-0.33 s)): The tunnel's centre hexagon is a torn hole. It expands to fill the frame in 3 drawings, while the next scene's flying subject (eagle) bursts out of it first. — RECIPE: Mask next scene by an irregular hexagon path (orange #E69B20 paper rim 3 % wide plus white torn edge). Radius steps: 35 %, 60 %, 95 %, 140 % of frame width. The leading subject sits above both layers and is already at 70 % scale on the first drawing.
- **Flying subject: replacement poses + cast shadow + camera roll** (both; 1.7 s scene: poses 4/2/3/2/8 drawings; roll snap at 6.58 s; pan decelerates): The subject is a series of different photo poses (frontal, side glide, banking, underside, top-down), each held 2-8 drawings. A flat blue-grey shadow copy sits on the ground layer, offset down-left. The background snaps into a -30° roll on one drawing, then pans diagonally while slowing. — RECIPE: Nano Banana Pro: a sticker sheet of the same flying object in 5 poses (cut out separately). Code: pose index from a tq lookup. Shadow = silhouette filled #3F6E9E at 45 % opacity, multiply, offset (-12 %, +18 %) of frame, blur 2. Background group rotates -8° to -30° in 1 drawing, then translates (-0.8 %, +1.2 %) per drawing with a 0.9x decay.
- **Rocket launch on a torn-paper streamer plume + tilt-up** (both; about 1.1 s; puffs grow over 4 drawings; scroll about 12 % of frame height per drawing): The character stays near the top-centre while the world scrolls down. 20-30 torn paper strips fan from the character down to the frame bottom, flapping. Photo cumulus puffs bloom from the horizon, then torn white cloud strips stream past. — RECIPE: Code: streamers are quads from the apex (x 50 %, y 25 %) to bottom points spread over 25-75 % of width. Each strip gets a random palette colour, a torn edge, and per-drawing ±3° and ±4 % length jitter. Puffs are NBP cumulus stickers scaled 0.3, 0.55, 0.8, 1.0. World layers translate +12 % H per drawing. Optional Seedance: a fluttering-streamer plate on a locked camera, posterised to twos.
- **Expo-out title landing (camera tilt deceleration)** (code; 9 drawings, 0.75 s. Bottom-edge y as a fraction of frame height: 0.075, 0.23, 0.36, 0.45, 0.53, 0.58, 0.63, 0.65, 0.67, 0.68): The title enters from above as part of the sky, decelerating geometrically to rest with no overshoot. — RECIPE: y(n) = yEnd - (yEnd - yStart)*0.78^n per drawing (or easeOutExpo over 0.75 s, sampled on twos). Move the whole sky layer, title and streamers together, so it reads as a camera tilt. A small flying cut-out appears from behind the title's top edge 2 drawings after it lands.
- **Faux-3D extruded print title** (both; holds 1.8 s): Static heavy flared-serif caps: red face, royal-blue extrusion receding up and outward toward a vanishing point above centre, 2-3 px white paper outline, watercolor texture with white specks. — RECIPE: Nano Banana Pro can render a short word like 'KAYE' directly as a title plate (see prompts). Code version: Cinzel 900 at about 13 % of frame height, leading 0.85. Draw the text 16 times, stepping toward the vanishing point (x 50 %, y -40 %), each step scale 0.995 and offset 0.6 px, filled #01429C with darker #023A84 every 4th step. Then draw the face #F3070F with a 3 px white stroke. Multiply a watercolor noise texture, and erase 2 % random specks to white.
- **Sticker page-peel reveal** (code; 6 drawings (0.42 s), ease-in-out, then 0.25 s hold): A horizontal band (y 30-70 %) peels left to right like a sticker. The curled flap shows a navy back with gradient shading, and a soft shadow falls on the revealed torn white strip carrying the wordmark. — RECIPE: Fold line x_f from 0 % to 105 % of width. Left of x_f: show the reveal layer. Right of x_f: show the original band. Flap: mirror the band slice [x_f, x_f + w] across x_f, compressed to 60 % width, filled #2A4479 with a linear gradient from #1E3466 at the fold to #3D5C99 at the edge, and a white torn edge on its outer side. Shadow: 25 px blur at 35 % black just left of the fold. Revealed strip: wood-grain white paper #E9E8E7 with torn top/bottom edges and a scrap ribbon below.
- **Multiverse logo style flicker** (both; 14 frames at 24 fps (0.58 s), holds 2-1-2-3-2-2-1-1, starting 0.67 s after the peel): The same wordmark at the same size and position cuts through 8 radically different styles for 1-3 frames each, then snaps back to the main lockup. — RECIPE: Make 8 Nano Banana Pro stills of 'kaye.creatives' in alt styles (VHS RGB glitch on black, torn street posters, flat green cartoon with yellow eyed blob clouds, orange woodgrain cardboard with doodle clouds, 80s chrome with blue lens flare, pink with blob confetti, neon gradient triangle, black RGB smear). ## Rhythm
Everything runs at 12 drawings/s on twos. There are 0 hard cuts in the first 13.8 s: the film is one continuous camera journey of 8 movement phases joined by zoom-throughs, a burst-through hole, a roll and a tilt. The only hard cuts are the 8 cuts of the logo flicker (13.83-14.42 s), held 2-1-2-3-2-2-1-1 frames at 24 fps, plus the cut back to the lockup.

Phase starts: 0.25 drop, 0.42 land, 2.67 push, 3.96 kaleidoscope, 5.92 burst, 6.58 roll snap, 7.92 roll-back, 9.42 launch, 10.58 title, 13.17 peel, 13.83 flicker, 14.42 final. Most phases last about 1.3-2.2 s, about one bar each at roughly 110-120 BPM. Accents land on landing drawings: boombox at 0.42, the roll snap, title settle at about 11.33.

Motion density alternates fast and slow. Fast stretches (pixel diff 40-90 per drawing) are the push (2.67-3.96), tunnel (3.96-5.92), eagle (5.92-7.0) and roll-back (7.92-8.6). Calm holds (diff 1-7) are 0.42-2.67 (2.25 s), 8.75-9.42 (0.67 s), 11.33-13.17 (1.84 s) and 14.42-15.02 (0.6 s). Inside the holds, small replacement pop-ups every 0.17-0.33 s keep the frame alive. The final 1.85 s (peel, hold, flicker, hold) carries the branding.

## Materials & light
Photo collage scanned under flat, shadowless light with no 3D shading anywhere. The only depth cues are soft drop shadows under cut-out layers (about 25-30 % dark, blur 6-10 px, offset down-right), the eagle's flat cast shadow on the ground layer, and the gradient-shaded page-peel curl.

Materials:
- Real photographs cut out with a clean white die-cut border: boombox, meerkats, eagle, hyrax, klipspringer, acacias, cumulus clouds. The rocks and bushes are desaturated to B&W or grey halftone.
- Hand-torn magazine paper with a fibrous white core on every edge, in flat saturated colours.
- Halftone dot screens and teal Memphis squiggle prints.
- Newsprint lorem-ipsum strips.
- Watercolor-painted cyan sky (a Waterlogue-style painterly filter, also applied to the title).
- White-washed wood grain under the logo, and red spray paint with overspray.

Grade: high saturation and contrast, whites near #F2F1F2 (no pure-white bloom), cyan sky dominant. There is a light paper grain, re-seeded every drawing, but no vignette and no film halation.
